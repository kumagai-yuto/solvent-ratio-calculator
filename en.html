About this tool

This tool helps adjust mixed solvent ratios used in column chromatography. For common systems such as hexane/ethyl acetate, dichloromethane/methanol, and toluene/ethyl acetate, it calculates how much solvent should be added to convert the current mixture into the desired target ratio.

The current ratio can be entered directly as a volume ratio or calculated from NMR integration values. When NMR integration is used, the tool converts the integration ratio into a volume ratio using molar mass, density, and the number of protons represented by the selected NMR signal.

The results include two preparation methods. Method 1 adds one solvent directly to the entire current mixture. Method 2 is an aliquot preparation method for cases where the required addition would become too large.

Notes

  • When using NMR integration values, the integrations must be corrected by the number of protons represented by each signal.
  • If peaks overlap or integrations are inaccurate, the calculated volume ratio may also be inaccurate.
  • This calculator assumes volume additivity and does not account for volume contraction on mixing.
  • Density depends on temperature and reagent grade. Adjust values according to the SDS or actual experimental conditions if needed.
